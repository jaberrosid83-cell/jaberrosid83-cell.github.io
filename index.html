SEO Explained

Key SEO Concepts

Understanding these pillars of SEO helps you make smarter decisions about your online presence.

Keyword Research

Finding the search terms your audience uses — covering volume, competition, and intent (informational, navigational, or transactional) to prioritize the right targets.

On-Page SEO

Optimizing content and HTML of individual pages: title tags, meta descriptions, heading hierarchy (H1–H3), keyword placement, image alt text, and content relevance.

Technical SEO

Backend optimizations helping crawlers access and index your site: page speed, mobile-friendliness, HTTPS, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, and structured data markup.

Core Web Vitals

Google's page experience signals: Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), Interaction to Next Paint (INP), and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) — all directly influencing rankings.

Off-Page SEO & Backlinks

Building authority through external links from reputable sites. Quality backlinks from relevant, high-authority domains signal trust to Google and boost rankings significantly.

E-E-A-T Signals

Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness — Google's quality framework. Demonstrating genuine expertise is critical in competitive and YMYL industries.
